# Association Profile — Design Direction v1.0

## Purpose
One-page opening profile for 山东大学学生科幻协会 in the “山口” section.

This is NOT:
- a history page;
- a photo wall;
- a recruitment poster;
- an AI-generated illustration page;
- a corporate dashboard.

Learn from the editorial discipline of the SCU and BIT anniversary magazines:
clear hierarchy, strong typography, direct labels, controlled asymmetry, and efficient use of space.
Do not copy their headings, slogans, layouts, colors, or motifs.

## Required content structure
Top:
- title: 山东大学学生科幻协会

Upper / middle information block:
- 协会名称 / 山东大学学生科幻协会
- 会徽 / place actual SDUSFA logo
- 创立时间 / 2006年10月14日
- 现有分会 / 中心 / 兴隆山 / 软件园
- 微信公众号 / SFW10422

Lower body:
- the two locked body paragraphs from `content/ASSOCIATION_PROFILE.json`

## Visual direction
- 185 × 260 mm, facing-page document system.
- Use existing warm-white paper/background behavior unchanged.
- Use existing Visual System: charcoal/black + restrained SDU red.
- Typography-driven page; no AI art and no new decorative illustration.
- Logo must be SMALL and subordinate, not a hero visual:
  target width 24–30 mm; hard maximum 32 mm.
- Prefer left-aligned title and information hierarchy.
- Labels may use SDU red or heavier sans serif.
- Values remain black/charcoal.
- Thin rules are allowed; avoid boxes/cards.
- Body text should occupy enough of the lower page to avoid a large dead zone.
- Body approx. 9.5–10 pt / 15–16 pt.
- Body width approx. 118–130 mm, or another clean measure consistent with the existing grid.
- Do not force a second page.
- Keep ordinary breathing room at bottom, but no decorative empty half-page.
- No large contour-line illustration. If any contour motif is used, it must be extremely subtle and non-competing; default is none.

## Editorial feel
Clean, concise, orderly.
The page should look like a real anniversary magazine profile page, not a Word document and not a generated poster.

## Copy lock
All visible wording in `content/ASSOCIATION_PROFILE.json` is final.
Do not rewrite, expand, shorten, normalize punctuation, or substitute institution names.

# Association Profile — Bottom Year Treatment / Scheme B

## Decision
Use Scheme B: warm-white background with oversized pale-red anniversary numerals.
Do NOT use a solid red footer band.

## Keep
Keep the current page's overall architecture from the latest user/Codex version:
- large title
- compact association information block
- small logo
- two body paragraphs
- one-page layout

Do not redesign the page from scratch.

## Bottom solution
Create a deliberate anniversary ending zone in the lower part of the page.

### Large year line
Text:
`2006—2026`

Placement:
- lower portion of page, below body copy
- visually centered on the page grid, but not necessarily mathematically page-centered
- baseline roughly in the 224–236 mm zone depending on body ending
- must stay inside the page and not collide with folio

Typography:
- Source Han Serif / 思源宋体 or the same display serif already used by the title
- approx. 34–42 pt
- regular/medium, not bold-black
- tracking slightly open if useful
- color: pale / desaturated SDU red
- target visual opacity equivalent approx. 28–40% of full accent strength
- no outline, shadow, glow, gradient

### Supporting line
Below the large years, add a thin SDU-red hairline:
- approx. 22–34 mm long
- 0.4–0.6 pt
- aligned to the same left edge or optical center as the supporting caption

Caption:
`山东大学学生科幻协会　二十周年`

- approx. 7.5–8.5 pt
- Source Han Sans / 思源黑体
- charcoal or restrained SDU red
- no English
- no slogan
- no additional facts

The caption is an editorial anniversary identifier, not body copy.

## Keywords row
Do NOT add the previously suggested activity keyword row.
The large-year ending should be the only new bottom element.
This keeps the page cleaner and prevents a "designed by AI" stacked-label effect.

## Body geometry
Adjust body only as needed so the final paragraph ends comfortably above the anniversary zone.
- body width target: 126–132 mm
- body text: approx. 9.6–10 pt / 15–16 pt
- both paragraphs share the same left edge
- first paragraph may retain a subtle red vertical rule if already present and visually balanced
- second paragraph should not become a full-width strip

Recommended spacing:
- body end to `2006—2026`: about 10–16 mm
- large year to supporting rule/caption: about 4–7 mm
- caption to bottom safe area: at least 10–12 mm

## Logo
Keep it subordinate:
- 20–24 mm preferred
- never larger than 24 mm in this revision

## Do not add
- photos
- contour lines
- star fields
- illustrations
- cards
- more labels
- English subtitles
- any extra slogan

## Overall feel
The page should finish with a strong but quiet anniversary cadence:
information → introduction → activity summary → `2006—2026`.

The bottom should feel intentionally occupied, not "filled".
